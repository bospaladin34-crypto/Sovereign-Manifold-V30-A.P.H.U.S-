### **1\. What "Up to 11 Dimensions" Actually Means in [PMC5467434](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5467434/)**

In [Reimann *et al.* (2017)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5467434/), "dimension" does **not** mean spatial coordinates—it is the **simplicial dimension $d=n-1$ of an acyclic all-to-all directed clique of $n$ neurons** in the **Directed Flag Complex** of the connectome:

* Because chemical synapses are unidirectional ($pre\rightarrow post$), 4 fully connected neurons have ${3}^{6}=729$ directed states and ${2}^{6}=64$ unidirectional tournaments, which split into **40 cyclic motifs** and **24 acyclic motifs ($4!=24$)**.  
* An **acyclic all-to-all clique of $n=d+1$ neurons** has a unique linear topological ordering \$v\_0 \\to v\_1 \\to \\dots \\to v\_d\$ where ${v}_{0}$ is the **unique source** ($de{g}_{in}=0,de{g}_{out}=d$), ${v}_{d}$ is the **unique sink** ($de{g}_{in}=d,de{g}_{out}=0$), and every neuron ${v}_{i}$ sends a direct feedforward skip-connection to every downstream neuron ${v}_{j}$ ($i<j$).  
* Topologically, this $n$\-neuron acyclic DAG is a **$d$\-dimensional polyhedron (${\Delta }^{d}$)**:  
  * In Blue Brain's 31,000\-neuron, 8-million\-synapse rat neocortical column ([Section 2.2.1 of PMC5467434](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5467434/)), they found **80 million directed 3-simplices** (4-neuron tetrahedra), directed simplices up to **dimension $d=7$ (8 neurons, 28 directed synapses)** inside a single column, **5-dimensional cavities (${\beta }_{5}>0$, enclosed by 6-neuron 5-simplices)**, and simplices reaching **dimension $d=11$ (12 neurons, 66 directed synapses, ${2}^{12}-1=4,095$ faces, and ${2}^{10}=1,024$ parallel feedforward paths from source to sink)** across larger cortical networks.
